# Social Media Do's
Maintain a professional online profile.
Think before posting content publicly.
Respect others and communicate politely online.
Verify information before sharing it.
Use privacy settings to protect personal information.


# Social Media Don'ts
Do not share personal details like address, phone number, or passwords.
Avoid spreading misinformation or fake news.
Do not post offensive or harmful comments.
Avoid clicking suspicious links or attachments.
Do not overshare sensitive academic or financial information.
