Cybercrime Case Study: Phishing Attack

Phishing is one of the most common forms of cybercrime in which attackers trick users into revealing sensitive information such as passwords, banking details, or OTPs. These attacks usually occur through fake emails, messages, or websites that appear to be from trusted organizations such as banks, payment services, or universities.

A typical phishing attack begins when a victim receives an email or message claiming that there is an urgent issue with their account. The message usually contains a link that redirects the user to a fake website designed to look identical to the original service. When the victim enters their login credentials or financial information on the fake website, the attacker immediately captures the data.

Students and young internet users are frequent targets because they often use multiple online platforms such as email, social media, and online payment applications. In India, phishing scams commonly involve fake messages related to bank verification, scholarship updates, or UPI payment alerts.

The consequences of phishing can be severe. Victims may lose access to their accounts, suffer financial loss, or have their personal information misused for further cybercrimes. In some cases, attackers may even gain access to institutional systems if compromised credentials belong to students or staff.

This highlights the importance of digital awareness and careful verification before clicking any links or sharing personal information online.
