# Stay Safe Online – Prevention Tips

Never share your OTP, passwords, or banking details with anyone online.
Always check the website URL carefully before logging in.
Avoid clicking links from unknown emails or messages.
Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on important accounts.
Use strong and unique passwords for different platforms.
Verify any UPI payment request before approving it.
Do not scan unknown QR codes for payments.
Keep your software and antivirus updated regularly.
Avoid using public Wi-Fi for financial transactions.
Immediately report suspicious activities.
